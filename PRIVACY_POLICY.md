# Privacy Policy — Thirteen Virtues

**Last Updated:** [02/09/2026]

## Overview

Thirteen Virtues ("the App") is a personal virtue-tracking app inspired by Benjamin Franklin's system of moral improvement. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.**

The App stores all data — including your daily check-ins, journal entries, and settings — locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account managed by Apple.

## What We Don't Do

- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not use tracking or advertising SDKs
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not have access to your data
- We do not require an account or login

## Data Storage

- **Local storage:** All data is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
- **iCloud sync (optional):** If enabled, data syncs through your personal iCloud account. Apple's iCloud privacy policy applies to this data.
- **Export:** You can export your data at any time as CSV or JSON files through Settings

## Data Deletion

You can delete all app data by uninstalling the App from your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, you may also need to remove the App's data from your iCloud storage through iOS Settings.

## Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications for evening review reminders. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App contains no inappropriate content and is suitable for all ages.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be noted with an updated date at the top.
